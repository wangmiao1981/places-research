## Massage Business Context
- Common service types: Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone, sports, Thai, reflexology, prenatal
- Licensing: California requires CMT (Certified Massage Therapist) certification
- Typical pricing: $60-120/hour depending on service type and location
- Key differentiators: specialization, ambiance, online booking, membership programs
- Common complaints: pressure too light/hard, cleanliness, wait times, upselling
- Industry growth: wellness industry growing ~5-10% annually
